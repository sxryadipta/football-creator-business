# Business Analysis of the Indian Football Creator Ecosystem

A small, reproducible analysis of 10 football YouTube channels: performance by segment, subscriber reach, content format, sponsorship evidence, and a hypothetical brand partnership proposal. Snapshot date **24 November 2026**.

## What changed in this version

- **Subscriber counts added** from public sources for 6 of 10 creators (previously none). Each observation is recorded with its source URL and as-of date in `data/raw/subscriber_observations_raw.csv`.
- **Sponsorship corrected.** The earlier version said sponsorship evidence existed for two creators. Verification shows SponsorRadar lists sponsors for four (Talk Football HD, Divyansh, YjR, and Markaroni, the last from the original research and not re-verified). Drog BABA shows no sponsors detected.
- **Proposal updated.** Divyansh is still recommended, but the earlier claim that it had no sponsor history was wrong. It has a listed sponsor history, so category conflicts must be checked.
- **Not changed:** video-level values. The watch pages could not be fetched during checking (rate limit and consent page), so likes, comments and missing dates remain N/A. Nothing was estimated.

## Key findings

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Subscriber counts verified | 6 of 10 (only Divyansh is current, 14 Nov 2026) |
| Median views, European-focused channels | 78,000 (n=12 videos) |
| Median views, Indian-focused channels | 18,000 (n=14) |
| Median views, Malayalam-language channel | 4,900 (n=3) |
| Median views per subscriber, verified channels | 0.13 to 0.54 |
| Channels with a listed sponsor | 4 of 10 (1 further with none detected) |
| Channels with a listed business contact | 1 of 10 (Divyansh) |

Caveats: the Indian segment's views-per-subscriber figure rests on one creator, and most subscriber counts are from 2025. These ratios are indicative, not benchmarks.

## Limitations

- **Subscriber gaps:** Playmaker India, Football Mania Malayalam, Curious Harry and Sharma Football have no verified count. These are left blank.
- **Dates differ:** subscriber counts range from 2024 to 2026. Four are more than 180 days old at the snapshot date.
- **Sources disagree:** for YjR the sources differ by about 50% (252,000 to 388,000), and for Markaroni by about 80% (250,000 to 450,000). The primary figure is the latest dated tracker value, and the full range is kept.
- **Video-level values unverified:** likes, comments and some dates remain N/A.
- **Sponsor counts are estimates:** SponsorRadar states its detections are deduced from video content, not confirmed by channels or brands.
- **Non-random sample** from search snippets. Results describe this snapshot only.
- **Analyst-assigned segments** based on title content.

Full list with row counts and handling: `data/processed/data_quality_log.csv`.

## Reproduce

```bash

./run_all.sh
```

`verify.py` recalculates the segment medians and overall medians from the raw video CSV using only the standard library, and checks them against the pipeline. Subscriber figures are taken directly from the source-recorded observations file.

## Method notes

- **Primary subscriber figure:** the latest dated value from a tracker (vidIQ, NexLev, SocialCounts). Where no dated value exists, an undated live counter is used and flagged. Modash and lower-bound bios are kept as observations but excluded from the primary figure.
- **Views per subscriber:** median analysed-video views divided by the primary subscriber count. Subscriber dates differ, so this is a rough reach ratio.
- **Analysis set:** rows with a title and view count, excluding one possible copy error.
